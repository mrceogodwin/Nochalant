# NOCHALANT CLUB — Product Requirements Document

**Product:** Nochalant Club  
**Tagline:** Welcome to the Club.  
**Positioning:** Your people. Your scene. Your rules.  
**Category:** 18+ social, lifestyle, music and culture community platform  
**Initial market:** Nigeria, with Lagos as an initial community launch market  
**Membership:** Hybrid — free membership with optional paid upgrades  
**Brand identity:** Black, white and red  
**Status:** Product direction locked; detailed specification for review and implementation

## 1. Executive summary
Nochalant Club is an 18+ digital social community where people discover communities, join conversations, create groups, exchange music, discuss nightlife, establish social identities and build networks. It is a collection of interconnected communities, not an entertainment blog, generic social clone or conventional music-streaming service.

Members join public or membership-based **Scenes**, create smaller private **Squads**, participate in real-time conversations, attend audio rooms, discover nightlife, submit music for community listening and develop identity through ranks, badges and achievements.

**Principles:** community before content; expressive membership; distinct community identities; easy conversation; status earned through contribution, not spending; genuine value for paid features; safety and privacy; premium mobile-app experience.

## 2. Objectives and success metrics
Build an active Nigerian adult community; encourage Scene and Squad participation; increase daily/weekly participation; establish music and lifestyle discovery; empower community leaders; create sustainable membership and creator revenue.

Measure acquisition (registrations), activation (first Scene joined within 24 hours), DAU/MAU, active Scenes and Squads, meaningful conversations, D7/D30 retention, eligible music submissions/listening sessions, paid conversion/recurring revenue and safety response times. Establish numerical targets after private beta; do not fabricate current performance.

## 3. Audience
Adults 18+: Gen Z adults and young millennials, music enthusiasts, independent artists/producers, nightlife/lifestyle enthusiasts, fashion and alternative-culture communities, students and young professionals, creatives and social conversationalists.

Personas: Culture Explorer, Scene Founder, Squad Captain, Music Head, Nightlife Insider, Social Butterfly and Artist.

## 4. Information architecture
Five primary destinations: **Home, Discover, Create, Chats, My Club ID**. Secondary destinations: Scenes, Squads, Voice Rooms, Listening Room, Nightlife, Notifications, Membership, Settings, Help and Safety.

Mobile-first bottom navigation; compact desktop navigation; contextual quick-create; persistent floating music player only where useful; consistent icons, responsive touch feedback and fast transitions.

## 5. Welcome and onboarding
Ordered flow: (1) Welcome — “WELCOME TO NOCHALANT CLUB.” / “Your people. Your scene. Your rules.” with Enter the Club and Sign in. (2) 18+ age eligibility; self-declaration may serve controlled beta, with proportionate assurance as risk/regulation/payment requirements dictate; never expose DOB. (3) Registration: display name, email, password, username, proportionate age information, Terms, Privacy and community guidelines acceptance; secure email verification and recovery; social sign-in later. (4) Interests: Music, Afrobeats, Alté, Nightlife, Fashion, Streetwear, Film, Nollywood, Gaming, Relationships, Lifestyle, Technology, Art, Photography, Travel, Campus, Entrepreneurship, Food and experiences. (5) Starter identity: Observer, Rebel, Music Head, Night Owl, Curator, Creative, Connector, Social One; identity is not rank. (6) Recommend first Scenes with join, details, save, and private access request. (7) Personalised welcome and Home.

## 6. Scenes
Scenes are public, private, paid or unlisted communities around interest, lifestyle, location, culture or activity. Paid and private are independent settings. Examples: Alté Society, Afterhours, Sound District, The Drip Room, Film Room, Soft Life Department, Lagos Unfiltered, No Sleep Club, Creative Misfits, Campus Frequency, Relationship Department, Listening Lounge.

Categories: Music, Lifestyle, Nightlife, Fashion, Entertainment, Film/TV, Creative arts, Technology, Relationships, Campus, Travel, Food/experiences, Gaming, General.

Creation fields: name, unique slug, description, category, cover, icon, visibility, membership rules, guidelines, optional location, fee, participation requirements consistent with 18+, welcome message and links.

Features: overview, feed, discussions/channels, member directory, events, voice rooms, polls, announcements, pinned content, rules, moderation, optional paid membership. Roles: Founder, Co-founder, Head Curator, Moderator, Event Host, Member. Founders retain ownership subject to platform enforcement/recovery. Discovery: search, categories, trending, recommendations, new, location, popular this week, friends/Squad activity. Ranking balances relevance, genuine activity, quality and safety; label paid placements.

## 7. Squads
Smaller private groups, optionally associated with a Scene. Creation fields: name, description, icon, cover, privacy, invitation policy, optional location and parent Scene. Privacy: invite-only, request-to-join, or discoverable to Scene members. Features: real-time chat, replies/reactions, voice notes, media, audio rooms, polls, pins, links, members, events, announcements, roles, removal, reporting, mute and leave. Roles: Founder, Captain, Co-captain, Moderator, Member. Ownership transfer has defined conditions. Squad admins cannot access unrelated private messages or bypass privacy.

## 8. Messaging
Direct messages subject to recipient privacy and message requests. Support text, images, links, voice notes, reactions, replies, controlled edit/delete, block/report. Scene channels (General, Music Talk, Introductions, Gossip, Recommendations, Announcements, Events, Off-topic) with admin create/archive/configure. Chat: real-time delivery, typing, configurable read receipts/online status, reactions, threads, mentions, pins, link previews, search, media gallery, reporting, rate limits and anti-spam. Voice notes need recording/playback/duration/progress/retry/validation/storage limits/reporting. Notification preferences for DMs, Squad, mentions, replies, follows, invites, events, Listening Room and payments.

## 9. Live Voice Rooms
Open, Scene, Squad, scheduled event, listening party, debate and artist feedback rooms. UI: title, host, speakers/listeners, speaking indicators, raise hand, mute, leave, invite, report and description. Roles: Host, Co-host, Speaker, Listener, Moderator. Host can approve speakers, mute/remove, lock/end room, control invitations and appoint co-hosts. Use LiveKit or equivalent; backend issues short-lived permission-checked tokens. No recording by default; future recording requires notice, consent and retention controls.

## 10. Club ID
Digital membership card: avatar, username, display name, Club ID, chosen identity, rank, reputation, achievements, Scenes, Squads, membership status, optional bio/social links. Privacy controls each public element. Example: NIGHTCURATOR / THE NIGHT OWL / SCENE CONNECTOR / 2,450 XP / AFTERHOURS FOUNDING MEMBER.

## 11. Ranks and reputation
Proposed ranks: New Face, Regular, Familiar, Vibe Curator, Scene Connector, Culture Keeper, Scene Legend, Icon, Grandmaster, Club Royalty. Configurable thresholds. Earn points for onboarding, joining Scenes, constructive participation, meaningful reactions, successful events, helping members and accepted music feedback. No points simply for purchases, paid submissions/placements, message volume, artificial engagement. Daily caps, duplicate detection, suspicious activity monitoring, account-age gates, review and fraud reversals. Reputation is not money and is not purchasable or transferable.

## 12. Icon and badge collection
Architecture supports at least 120 original Nochalant artworks and future drops. Catalogues: Royalty (1–10), Nightlife (11–20), Music (21–30), Alternative Culture (31–40), Fashion (41–50), Social (51–60), Creative (61–70), Mystery (71–80), Competitive (81–90), Exclusive/Seasonal (91–100), additional identities (101–120). Names include Crown, Royal Seal, Night Owl, Afterhours, Neon Pulse, DJ Deck, Music Head, Sound Architect, Rebel, Phantom, Misfit, Drip, Sneakerhead, Connector, Squad Captain, Artist, Film Director, Observer, Oracle, Champion, Founding Member, Beta Pioneer, Scene Founder, Culture Ambassador, City Explorer, Foodie, Film Head, Gamer, Tech Head, Debate Champion, Listening Room Regular and Club Royalty. Each icon: unique ID, name, description, category, original artwork, rarity, unlock condition, optional season, active state. Rarity: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Founder’s Edition. Members choose one primary identity and multiple secondary badges. No emoji as final artwork.

## 13. Listening Room
Music discovery and community feedback; submissions initially link to Spotify, Apple Music, Audiomack, YouTube, SoundCloud and approved services. A link is not an upload/hosting licence. Required metadata: artist, title, external link, genre, description, cover artwork rights, category and rights declaration. Formats: First Listen, Rate My Track, Producer’s Corner, The Drop, Battle Room. Flow: submit metadata, choose free/paid eligibility, accept rules, pay if applicable, moderation, queue approval, external playback, feedback, artist summary. Paid review buys a defined review opportunity, never guaranteed streams, positive feedback, votes or chart position; label paid placement. Interactions: external play, text/voice feedback, reactions, eligible votes, save, follow, share, report. Artist dashboard: status, reviews, feedback, eligible metrics, receipts, profile, links. Anti-abuse: rate limits, duplicate detection, fake engagement prevention, moderation, copyright complaints/takedowns.

## 14. Artist accounts
Profiles: name, image, cover, bio, genre, optional location, music/social links, releases, submissions, followers, discussions. Types: independent artist, producer, DJ, group, label/representative. Verification requires evidence; payment never buys verification. Dashboard edits profile, submits/manages tracks, sees feedback/eligible metrics and promotes approved content.

## 15. Nightlife
Discover clubs/lounges/events by location; share experiences; rate venues; discuss DJs/performances; event discussions/flyers (rights checks); nightlife Squads; save/follow. Venue data: name, location, description, photos, operating info, official contacts/socials, events, reviews. Separate ratings: music, atmosphere, service, value, overall. Eligible reviews only; detect fake/coordinated ratings. No default live-location exposure or private event addresses without permission; safe meetup guidance; label user-generated vs venue-verified information.

## 16. Home feed
Aggregate relevant Scene discussions, permitted Squad activity, music submissions, polls, events, member posts, trending conversations, artist announcements and room invitations. Actions: react, comment/reply, share, save, follow, report, hide, mute. Rank by interests, joined Scenes, follows, recent activity, quality, freshness and safety; avoid outrage/spam incentives.

## 17. Events
Scene/Squad events: title, description, date/time, venue or online room, host, capacity, access, RSVP, optional ticket link. RSVP, share, reminders, announcements, attendee count, cancellation, reporting. Initially link external ticketing rather than process tickets.

## 18. Membership and monetisation
Hybrid free + optional upgrades. Free: Club ID, public discovery, eligible public Scenes, eligible Squads, standard messaging, participation, basic icons/profile, Listening Room participation and nightlife discovery. Inner Circle hypothesis ₦1,500/month: exclusive Scenes, themes, identity customisation, expanded Squad tools/community capacity, seasonal icons, saved content. Black Card hypothesis ₦5,000/month: Inner Circle plus premium experiences/events/collections/eligible priority access. Prices are hypotheses, not commitments. Other revenue: paid Scenes, Scene subscriptions, Listening Room fees/sponsored showcases, labelled placements, venue promos, event partnerships, creator tools, brand campaigns. Paystack or approved Nigerian provider; one-time/recurring where supported, server-verified payment, webhooks, receipts, refunds, expiry, cancellation and history. Never grant entitlements from browser redirect alone.

## 19. Trust, safety and moderation
Prohibit harassment, threats, targeted abuse, doxxing, non-consensual intimate imagery, exploitation, impersonation, fraud, scams, spam, copyright infringement, illegal content, hate-based harassment, manipulation and dangerous meetup behaviour. Report accounts/messages/posts/Scenes/Squads/rooms/submissions/events/reviews. Workflow: report, case, evidence retained per policy, risk, review, action, notice, appeal where appropriate, close/audit. Enforcement: warning, removal, posting/messaging restriction, suspension, permanent suspension, community removal/restriction. Member controls: block, mute, restrict messages, hide profile, report, leave, disable discoverability, online/profile visibility. Super Admin manages platform, members, cases, communities, memberships, payments, icons, artists, venues/events and audit logs. Moderators act only within assigned permissions. Log all privileged actions.

## 20. Notifications
In-app, push and essential email for DMs, Squad, mentions, replies, invitations, follows, room invites, events, feedback, submission approval, renewals, receipts and moderation outcomes. Configurable non-essential notifications.

## 21. Search
Global search for members, discoverable Scenes/Squads, artists, submissions, posts, events, venues and tags. Enforce visibility and membership permissions; never expose private content.

## 22. Admin workspace
Separate RBAC-protected admin for members, Scenes, Squads, moderation, voice rooms, Listening Room, artists, venues, events, plans, payments, icon catalogue, notifications, configuration and audit logs. Overview includes registrations, activity, communities, messages, submissions, revenue, failures and cases.

## 23. Technical architecture
Target: Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind/reusable UI, Vercel, Neon PostgreSQL, Prisma, Auth.js or validated Neon Auth, managed realtime/WebSocket, LiveKit, S3-compatible storage, Paystack, Resend, privacy-conscious analytics, Sentry. Existing repo is React/Vite; migration to Next.js is planned, not assumed. First audit code, auth, schema and reusable features. No Supabase. Server-side permission checks, strict community isolation, transactional financial operations, signed media URLs, short-lived voice tokens, rate limits, idempotent webhooks, audit logs, background jobs, reviewed migrations. Persist durable messages/events in PostgreSQL; use realtime infrastructure for distribution/presence/typing/audio, not DB polling.

## 24. Proposed data model
Identity: users, user_profiles, user_settings, user_interests, user_sessions, user_blocks, user_follows, age_assurance_records. Membership: membership_plans, memberships, membership_entitlements, payment_transactions, payment_webhook_events, refunds. Scenes: scenes, scene_members, scene_roles, scene_channels, scene_posts, scene_rules, scene_invitations. Squads: squads, squad_members, squad_roles, squad_messages, squad_invitations. Messaging: conversations, conversation_members, messages, message_attachments, message_reactions, message_reports. Voice: voice_rooms, voice_room_members, voice_room_events, voice_room_moderation. Identity/rewards: icon_catalog, badge_catalog, user_icons, user_badges, reputation_events, rank_definitions, user_rank_history, achievement_definitions, user_achievements. Music: artist_profiles, music_submissions, music_submission_reviews, music_feedback, music_votes, music_submission_payments. Nightlife: venues, venue_claims, venue_reviews, nightlife_events, event_rsvps, venue_reports. Safety: moderation_cases, moderation_actions, content_reports, appeals, audit_logs. Notifications: notifications, notification_preferences, notification_delivery_logs. Define ownership, membership, constraints, indexes and permissions for every private entity before implementation.

## 25. Security and privacy
Enforce 18+, secure credentials/session management, email verification, server-side authz, private message and community isolation, TLS, webhook protection, least-privilege admins, audit logs, account deletion/export where required, media retention and applicable Nigerian privacy obligations. Never publicly expose email, DOB, private member lists or conversations.

## 26. Performance and reliability
Fast initial load, responsive nav, safe optimistic UI, reliable message delivery, network recovery, upload progress/retries, image resizing, lazy loading, feed pagination, accessible controls and mobile-network performance. Payments, membership and moderation cannot rely on optimistic UI.

## 27. UI and brand
**Black, white and Nochalant red** (logo-inspired), charcoal/graphite/muted grey. Premium app-like design, distinctive type, high-contrast compact cards, custom iconography, restrained transitions, tactile states, consistent sheets/modals, skeletons, helpful empty/error states. Optional floating player must not dominate community experience.

## 28. Phased delivery
Phase 0 audit: repo, Neon, auth/data, migration/rollback, design system, preview env, test strategy. Phase 1 Club foundation: welcome, auth, age, profiles/Club ID, interests/icons, discovery, public/private Scenes. Phase 2 community: Scene creation/membership/channels, Squads/invites/roles/feed/basic moderation. Phase 3 messaging: DMs, realtime Scene/Squad chat, reactions/replies/media/voice notes/notifications. Phase 4 identity: ranks, reputation, 120 icons, badges/achievements/leaderboards/anti-abuse. Phase 5 live: voice rooms, Listening Room, artists, feedback, nightlife, events. Phase 6 monetisation: free/paid tiers, paid Scenes/submissions, artist tools, promotions, payments/refunds. Phase 7 scale: performance, discovery, moderation, recommendations, install experience, analytics and geographic expansion.

## 29. MVP acceptance
Adult registration/sign-in; onboarding and Club ID; discover/join public Scene; request private Scene; authorised Scene creation; Squad creation and permission enforcement; real-time messaging; report/block; moderator case resolution; icon choice/earning; anti-manipulated reputation; server-verified payments and entitlements; private data isolation; mobile/desktop; safe network/payment recovery; auditable admin actions. Voice, advanced music and paid events require separate acceptance tests. Screens alone do not constitute release readiness.

## 30. Launch
Controlled adult private beta; founding Scenes: Alté Society, Afterhours, Sound District, The Drip Room, Lagos Unfiltered, Creative Misfits, No Sleep Club, Film Room. Recruit hosts/moderators/founding members. Founding Member badge rewards early participation, not payment. Public launch only after security, moderation, guidelines, payment, support, privacy and terms readiness.

## 31. Locked decisions
Nochalant Club brand; hybrid membership; 18+; Scenes, Squads, chat and Club ID as core; 120-icon architecture; reputation/ranks; Neon PostgreSQL; Vercel preferred; Next.js target architecture; Paystack or equivalent proposed. Voice, Listening Room, artist, nightlife are planned modules. Existing production migration is not authorised by this PRD.

## 32. Vision
Nochalant Club is somewhere to find your people, stay for conversations, build a Squad, lead a Scene, discover artists, attend events and develop a recognisable identity without needing to become an influencer.

**NOCHALANT CLUB. Your people. Your scene. Your rules. Welcome to the Club.**
